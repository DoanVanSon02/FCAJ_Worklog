## Week 7 Objectives:

- Understand the role of Amazon CloudWatch in resource monitoring.
- Learn about Metric, Dashboard, and Alarm.
- Practice monitoring EC2 and AWS Backup metrics.
- Configure alerts for successful and failed backup jobs.
