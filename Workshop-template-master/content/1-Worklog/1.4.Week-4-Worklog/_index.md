## Week 4 Objectives:

- Understand the roles of Elastic Load Balancing and EC2 Auto Scaling.
- Learn about Application Load Balancer, Target Group, and Health Check.
- Practice distributing traffic between EC2 instances.
- Practice managing the number of EC2 instances using an Auto Scaling Group.
