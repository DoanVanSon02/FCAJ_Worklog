## Week 1 Objectives:

- Get to know the members and the internship program rules.
- Understand the AWS overview and basic service categories.
- Become familiar with the AWS Management Console and AWS CLI.
- Learn and practice basic access management with IAM.

## Week 1 Achievements:

- Understood the internship rules and roadmap.
- Gained an overview of AWS, Regions, and Availability Zones.
- Learned how to use the AWS Console to search for and access services.
