## Week 2 Objectives:

- Understand the role of Amazon VPC and its basic networking components.
- Distinguish between Public Subnet and Private Subnet.
- Learn about Internet Gateway, Route Table, and Security Group.
- Practice building a basic network to prepare for EC2 deployment.

- Understood the functions of VPC, Subnet, Internet Gateway, and Route Table.
